How is the leaderboard computed?

Every public /r/{job_id} report flows automatically into this board, grouped by base_url domain. Ranking uses Bayesian-weighted scoring (prior=50, prior_weight=5): a relay with one perfect 100 scores only 58.3; it takes ≥10 stable near-perfect tests to climb to the top of the main board. This prevents "a brand-new relay runs once, gets lucky 100, and dominates the board" — relays that truly withstand repeated testing rank first. The "median" shown in the UI is the median of all historical scores (an intuitive reference), while ordering follows the Bayesian weighting.

Relays tested only once do not enter the main board (to avoid noise), but remain visible at the bottom of the full list.
